The Tetris game at tetris/tetris.html has a bug that freezes the whole page.

Reproduce: load the page and press the Up arrow to rotate. The tab locks up and stops responding to any further input. Verified with a headless browser: after the rotate keydown, the page never answers again.

The cause is the ghost-piece loop in the render function:

    let gy = 0;
    while (!collides(current, 0, gy + 1)) gy++;

That loop has no upper bound and runs every frame. After some rotations the exit condition is never met and it spins forever.

Fix it by bounding that loop to at most ROWS iterations, and make tryRotate reject a rotation whose resulting shape would sit outside the board rather than leaving the piece in a state the ghost loop cannot resolve.

Use edit_file to change only what is needed. Do not rewrite the whole file. Then stop.
